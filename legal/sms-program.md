# Margo SMS notifications

**Operated by KMO Solutions Foundry LLC** ([kmosolutionsfoundry.com](https://kmosolutionsfoundry.com))

**Effective date:** November 2, 2026

This page describes the text-message program for **Margo**, the personal chief-of-staff assistant that
KMO Solutions Foundry LLC operates for its founder, Ceryce Armstrong. Margo's messages are sent from
**(618) 726-8723**.

## Who receives messages

Exactly one person: **the account owner**, Ceryce Armstrong, at the mobile number she enrolled herself.
There is no public sign-up form, no mailing list, and no third-party opt-in. Margo does not text
customers, callers, or anyone else — including people who call or text (618) 726-8723 — and it never
sends marketing or promotional content.

## How the owner opts in

The owner opts in herself, in two steps:

1. **Save the number.** She saves her own mobile number as the sole SMS recipient in Margo's private,
   owner-only settings.
2. **Text START.** From that same mobile, she texts **START** to **(618) 726-8723**.

## Consent language

By completing both steps, the owner agrees to the following:

> I agree to receive recurring automated texts from Margo (KMO Solutions Foundry LLC) at this number:
> reminders I set, alerts I request, and replies to my texts. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates
> may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Margo confirms the opt-in with this reply:

> Margo (KMO Solutions Foundry LLC): You're subscribed to owner alerts & reminders. Msg frequency
> varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel.

## What Margo sends

- **Reminders** the owner scheduled herself — appointments, deadlines, and to-dos.
- **Alerts** she asked for — the result of a call screened on the Margo number, or a transcribed
  voicemail. These can include the caller's callback number or a link to the recording.
- **Replies** to texts she sends to the Margo number.
- An optional **morning brief** of what needs her attention that day.

Example messages:

- `Margo: Reminder: team standup at 10:00. Reply STOP to opt out.`
- `Margo: Missed call from (555) 010-0199 about Thursday's meeting. They asked for a callback.`
- `Margo: Voicemail from (555) 010-0199: "Following up on the proposal — call me back when you can."`
- `Margo: Done — moved your project review to Friday 9:00 AM. I'll text you an hour before.`
- `Margo: Morning brief — 2 things need you today: review the draft, reply to the vendor. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.`

## Message frequency

Message frequency varies with the owner's own schedule and requests — typically a few messages per day.

## Cost

**Msg & data rates may apply** according to your mobile carrier plan.

## Opting out

Reply **STOP** at any time to opt out (STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPTOUT, and REVOKE also
work). Margo sends one final confirmation and nothing after it:

> Margo (KMO Solutions Foundry LLC): You're unsubscribed and will get no more texts. Reply START to
> resubscribe.

## Help

Reply **HELP** (or **INFO**) for help, or email **contact@ceryce.com**. Margo replies:

> Margo (KMO Solutions Foundry LLC): owner-only alerts & reminders. Help: contact@ceryce.com. Msg & data
> rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel.

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
