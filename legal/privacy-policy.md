# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **KMO Solutions Foundry LLC** ("KMO Solutions Foundry," "we," "us," or
"our"; [kmosolutionsfoundry.com](https://kmosolutionsfoundry.com)) collects, uses, and protects
information in connection with the website at **ceryce.com** — the personal site of our founder, Ceryce
Armstrong — and the personal-assistant services ("Margo") we operate for her, which screen and relay
phone calls and email and send her text-message notifications (together, the "Services").

By using the Services — including by calling, texting, or emailing a number or address we operate, or by
interacting with the Margo assistant — you agree to this Policy.

## Information we collect

- **Information you provide.** Your name, phone number, email address, and the contents of any call,
  text message, voicemail, or email you send to a number or address we operate, including any details you
  choose to share when the Margo assistant asks who you are and why you're calling.
- **Communications metadata.** Call and message timestamps, caller/sender number, duration, and
  delivery status, used to screen and route communications.
- **Technical and usage data.** Standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages
  viewed) when you visit ceryce.com, and limited cookies strictly necessary to operate the site.

We do not knowingly collect special categories of sensitive personal information through the Services,
and we ask that you not send them.

## How we use information

- To answer, screen, and route phone calls and messages, and to take and relay messages to Ms. Armstrong.
- To respond to your inquiries and communicate with you.
- To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Services.
- To detect and prevent spam, fraud, and abuse.
- To comply with legal obligations.

## Text messaging (SMS)

Margo's text-message program is **owner-only**. Margo sends text messages to exactly one person: the
account owner, Ceryce Armstrong, at the mobile number she enrolled herself by saving it in Margo's private,
owner-only settings and texting **START** to **(618) 726-8723**. Those messages are reminders she set,
alerts she requested (such as the result of a screened call or a transcribed voicemail), replies to her
own texts, and an optional morning brief. They are operational messages, not marketing. Message frequency
varies. **Msg & data rates may apply.** Reply **STOP** to opt out and **HELP** for help. Program details
are at [ceryce.com/sms](https://ceryce.com/sms), and the full messaging terms are in the
[Terms & Conditions](https://ceryce.com/terms).

Margo does not send text messages to anyone else. If you are not the account owner and you call or text a
number we operate, you are not enrolled in any messaging program and Margo will not text you.

**No mobile information (including phone numbers and SMS/text-messaging opt-in or consent data) will be
sold, rented, or shared with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional
purposes.** Any data sharing described below specifically **excludes** text-messaging originator opt-in
data and consent; that information is never shared with third parties.

## How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

- **With service providers** who process it solely to operate the Services on our behalf — for example,
  our telephony/SMS provider (Twilio), AI processing used to power the assistant (Anthropic), and
  hosting, email, and storage providers. These providers are permitted to use the information only to
  provide their services to us.
- **For legal reasons** — to comply with applicable law, legal process, or enforceable governmental
  request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of ourselves or others.

## Data retention

We keep call and message logs, transcripts, and related records only as long as needed to provide and
improve the Services, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations, after which they are deleted or
de-identified.

## Your choices and rights

- **Opt out of texts** any time by replying **STOP**.
- **Access, correction, or deletion.** You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about
  you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to legal limits.
- Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below.

## Cookies

ceryce.com uses only the minimal cookies necessary for the site to function. We do not use advertising or
cross-site tracking cookies.

## Children's privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do
not knowingly collect their information.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of
transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Effective date" above reflects the latest version, and
